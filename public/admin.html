Required CSV Format

Your CSV needs these column headers in the first row (product_code, product_name, category and price are required; the rest are optional): 

product_code,product_name,category,brand,price,offer_price,stock,unit,image
RIC001,Aashirvaad Rice,Rice,Aashirvaad,650,599,30,5 kg,

Tip: if your product list is currently a Word document, open it in Word, select the table, and use "Save As" → CSV, or copy it into Excel/Google Sheets and export as CSV. Products with a product_code that already exists will be updated automatically instead of duplicated.
